**EXP-06** *(updated 2026-09-07)*
- Hypothesis: Reusing an already-published proof example measurably underperforms a fresh, previously-unpublished example within the same content structure, for this account.
- Reason (original, 2026-08-26): POST-011 reused the café example from TOPIC-018 and reached 52 impressions, lining up with LinkedIn's official March 2026 statement that it downranks "recycled thought leadership posts."
- New Evidence (2026-09-07): POST-015 reused the TOPIC-011 agent-failure story for the third time on this account (POST-009 original, POST-013 as a credibility anchor, now POST-015 in full) and settled at 22 impressions — the account's lowest-ever result, lower even than POST-011's 52. This is now 2 retrospective data points (POST-011: 52, POST-015: 22) both showing severe underperformance associated with reused proof, though POST-015 is confounded with the new DNA-05 structure question (see EXP-10) and cannot be attributed to reuse alone.
- Variable: Reused vs. fresh proof example.
- Control: Same DNA structure, standard (non-deviating) hook pattern, on-brand visual family — future tests should isolate reuse from structure/hook changes.
- Sample Size: 2 retrospective data points; target 2+ prospectively with a clean (non-DNA-05, non-stat-hook) post still needed to isolate reuse specifically.
- Confidence: Strengthening (Experimental → leaning Emerging) — the underlying mechanism (LinkedIn's official recycled-content downranking) is OFFICIAL-tier; this account now has two low results associated with reuse, but neither is a clean isolated test.
- Next Action: Do not reuse any previously-published proof example for at least the next 4-5 posts — treat this as a hard rule rather than a hypothesis still being tested, given two increasingly severe results.
- Status: ACTIVE

**EXP-10** *(new 2026-09-07, opened by linkedin-learning-engine from POST-015's review)*
- Hypothesis: DNA-05 (honest-failure/postmortem structure) underperforms this account's other structures regardless of proof quality or visual treatment.
- Reason: Both DNA-05 posts on record — POST-009 (82 impressions) and POST-015 (22 impressions) — are the account's two lowest results. Both used real, specific proof and full on-brand visuals (VISUAL-08), which are otherwise associated with stronger performance elsewhere in the account. This is now the cleanest structure-level weak signal in the account, though POST-015 is also confounded by proof reuse (EXP-06) and a stat-first hook, so the postmortem structure itself cannot yet be blamed in isolation.
- Variable: Content structure (DNA-05 postmortem vs. DNA-02 case-study vs. DNA-03 opinion/commentary).
- Control: Real named proof, on-brand visual, zero hashtags, held constant.
- Test: Retire DNA-05 for at least the next 3-4 posts. Route any future "what broke" material through DNA-02 (Strong) or DNA-03 (Experimental but higher-evidence) instead, and compare.
- Sample Size: 2 retrospective data points (both negative); none planned prospectively since the recommendation is to stop using this structure rather than keep testing it.
- Confidence: Emerging — n=2 is thin, but both points point the same direction and are the account's two worst results by a wide margin.
- Next Action: Treat DNA-05 as a documented weak structure for this account going forward. Re-open only if a future post using it, with fresh proof and a non-stat-first hook, breaks the pattern.
- Status: ACTIVE
